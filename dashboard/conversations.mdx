# Managing Conversations

The Conversations view (Inbox) is where you monitor and respond to customer messages. This powerful interface combines AI automation with human expertise to deliver exceptional customer support.

## Inbox Layout

The inbox uses a three-pane layout for efficient conversation management:

### Left Panel: Conversation List

Browse all conversations with key information:
- **Visitor Name/Email**: Identify who you're talking to
- **Last Message Preview**: Quick context about the conversation
- **Timestamp**: When the last message was sent
- **Unread Badge**: Indicates new messages
- **AI Status Indicator**: Shows if AI is active or paused

### Center Panel: Message Thread

View the full conversation history:
- **Chronological Messages**: All messages in order
- **Message Author**: Clearly labeled (Visitor, AI Agent, Team Member)
- **Timestamps**: Precise message timing
- **Rich Content**: Images, files, and formatted text
- **Internal Notes**: Private messages visible only to your team (marked with a lock icon)

### Right Panel: Visitor Details

Access visitor information and actions:
- **Visitor Profile**: Name, email, company
- **Device Information**: Browser, OS, location
- **Visit History**: Pages viewed, time on site
- **Conversation Actions**: Resolve, archive, mark as spam

## Conversation Statuses

Conversations move through different statuses based on their lifecycle:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open">
    Active conversations requiring attention. These appear by default in your inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolved">
    Successfully closed conversations. Mark a conversation as resolved when the issue is handled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archived">
    Older conversations removed from active view but still accessible via search.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spam">
    Filtered conversations marked as suspicious or unwanted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Responding to Conversations

### Sending Messages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a Conversation">
    Click on any conversation from the list to open it
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type Your Message">
    Use the message composer at the bottom of the thread
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Visibility">
    Select **Public** (visible to visitor) or **Private** (internal note for team)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send or Add Attachments">
    Press Enter or click Send. Use the attachment button to add files or images.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Press `Ctrl + Enter` (or `Cmd + Enter` on Mac) to send messages quickly without clicking the button.
</Tip>

### Message Types

**Public Messages**
- Visible to the visitor
- Display your name and avatar
- Sent with optional file attachments
- Can include images, documents, or links

**Private Notes**
- Only visible to your team members
- Perfect for internal discussion or context sharing
- Marked with a lock icon
- Help coordinate responses without confusing visitors

## AI Agent Controls

Manage how your AI assistant handles each conversation:

### Pause AI Answers

Temporarily stop the AI from responding:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Conversation Menu">
    Click the three-dot menu in the conversation header
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select 'Pause AI'">
    Choose the pause duration (10 minutes, 1 hour, or indefinitely)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take Over">
    Handle the conversation manually while AI is paused
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
When AI is paused, a banner appears at the top of the conversation showing when it will resume.
</Note>

### Resume AI Answers

Re-enable AI responses:
1. Click the "Resume AI" button in the conversation header
2. The AI agent will start responding to new messages automatically

### Join Escalation

When a visitor requests human help:
- Your AI agent detects escalation requests automatically
- A notification appears in the conversation
- Click "Join to Help" to take over the conversation
- The visitor sees a message that you've joined

## Conversation Actions

### Mark as Resolved

Close a conversation when the issue is handled:
1. Click the **Resolve** button in the conversation header
2. The conversation moves to the Resolved filter
3. If the visitor sends a new message, it automatically reopens

### Mark as Spam

Filter out unwanted conversations:
1. Open the conversation menu (three dots)
2. Select **Mark as Spam**
3. The conversation moves to the Spam filter
4. To undo, use **Mark as Not Spam**

### Archive Conversation

Remove conversations from active view:
1. Open the conversation menu
2. Select **Archive**
3. Find archived conversations using the Archive filter
4. Unarchive anytime by selecting **Unarchive**

### Update Conversation Title

Customize conversation names for easy identification:
1. Click the conversation title at the top
2. Enter a new title (e.g., "Billing Question - John Doe")
3. Press Enter to save
4. The AI generates titles automatically, but you can override them

## Typing Indicators

Stay informed about activity in real-time:

- **Visitor Typing**: See "Visitor is typing..." when they're composing a message
- **Team Member Typing**: See which team member is responding
- **Your Typing Status**: Other team members see when you're typing

<Tip>
Typing indicators help prevent duplicate responses when working with a team.
</Tip>

## Sound Notifications

Stay alert to new messages:

### Notification Settings

Configure sound alerts in Settings > Notifications:
- **New Message Sound**: Play audio when new messages arrive
- **Do Not Disturb**: Mute all sounds temporarily
- **Desktop Notifications**: Enable browser notifications for new conversations

## Filtering and Search

### Conversation Filters

Quickly find conversations by status:
- Click **Open**, **Resolved**, **Archived**, or **Spam** in the left sidebar
- The conversation list updates instantly
- Unread count badges show pending conversations

### Search Conversations

Find specific conversations:
1. Use the search bar at the top
2. Search by visitor name, email, or message content
3. Results appear as you type
4. Press `Esc` to clear search

Collaborate privately within conversations:

<Steps>

  </Step>

## Hard Limits and Restrictions

<Warning>
Depending on your plan, conversation access may be limited:
- **Free Plan**: Access last 30 days of conversations (older conversations are locked)
- **Paid Plans**: Unlimited conversation history access
- **Message Limits**: Free plans may have monthly message caps
</Warning>

When a conversation is locked:
- A lock icon appears next to the conversation
- You can view messages but cannot send new ones
- Upgrade your plan to unlock full access

## Troubleshooting

### Messages Not Appearing

- Check your internet connection
- Refresh the page to reconnect WebSocket
- Verify conversation filters aren't hiding messages

### AI Not Responding

- Check if AI is paused for this conversation
- Verify AI agent is active in settings
- Ensure knowledge base is trained

### Can't Send Messages

- Check if conversation is locked due to plan limits
- Verify you have access to this website
- Ensure message isn't empty
