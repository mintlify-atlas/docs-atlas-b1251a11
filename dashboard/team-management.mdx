# Team Management

Cossistant enables seamless team collaboration on customer support. Invite colleagues, assign roles, and work together to deliver exceptional customer experiences.

## Team Settings Overview

Access team management from **Settings > Team** in your dashboard. Here you can:

- View all team members with access
- Manage pending invitations
- Monitor seat usage against your plan limits
- Control member roles and permissions

## Inviting Team Members

Add collaborators to your Cossistant workspace:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Team Settings">
    Click **Settings** in the top navigation, then select **Team**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite">
    Click the "Invite" button in the top-right corner
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Email Address">
    Provide the email address of the person you want to invite
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Access Level">
    Select organization-wide or website-specific access
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send Invitation">
    Click "Send Invite" - they'll receive an email with a join link
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Invited users receive an email with a secure link to join your organization. Invitations expire after 7 days.
</Note>

## Team Roles and Permissions

Cossistant has three role levels with different permissions:

### Owner

The account creator with full control:

- **Full Access**: All websites and features
- **Billing**: Manage subscription and payment methods
- **Team Management**: Add/remove any members, change roles
- **Settings**: Configure all organization and website settings
- **Deletion**: Can delete websites and close account

<Warning>
Owner role cannot be transferred or removed. Each organization has exactly one owner.
</Warning>

### Admin

Organization-level administrators:

- **All Websites**: Access to all websites in the organization
- **Team Management**: Invite members and manage roles (except owner)
- **Settings**: Configure websites and AI agents
- **Conversations**: View and respond to all conversations
- **Limited Billing**: View billing information (cannot modify)

### Member

Standard team member with scoped access:

- **Assigned Websites**: Only access specific websites they're invited to
- **Conversations**: View and respond to conversations
- **AI Agent**: View AI agent settings (cannot modify)
- **No Team Management**: Cannot invite others or change roles
- **No Billing**: Cannot view or modify billing information

## Managing Team Members

### Viewing Current Members

The **Members with Access** section shows:

- **Profile**: Avatar, name, and email address
- **Role**: Current permission level (Owner, Admin, Member)
- **Access Scope**: Organization-wide or website-specific
- **Last Seen**: When they last accessed the dashboard
- **Online Status**: Green indicator shows currently active members

### Changing Member Roles

Update permissions for team members:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Member">
    Locate the team member in the Members list
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Actions Menu">
    Click the three-dot menu next to their name
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select New Role">
    Choose "Make organization admin" or "Make organization member"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Change">
    Confirm the role change in the dialog that appears
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Only Admins and the Owner can change member roles. You cannot change the Owner's role or your own role.
</Note>

### Removing Team Members

Revoke access when someone leaves your team:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Member Menu">
    Click the three-dot menu next to the member's name
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Remove Access">
    Click "Remove access" (displayed in red)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Removal">
    Confirm the action in the dialog that appears
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Removing a member is immediate and they'll lose access to all conversations and settings. This action cannot be undone (but you can re-invite them).
</Warning>

## Managing Invitations

### Viewing Pending Invitations

The **Invitations** section shows all sent invites:

- **Email Address**: Who the invitation was sent to
- **Status**: Pending, Accepted, Declined, or Expired
- **Sent By**: Team member who sent the invitation
- **Expiration**: When the invitation will expire

### Invitation Statuses

- **Pending**: Invitation sent, awaiting response
- **Accepted**: User has joined your organization
- **Declined**: User rejected the invitation
- **Expired**: Invitation expired after 7 days
- **Canceled**: Invitation was manually canceled

### Resending Invitations

If an invitation wasn't received or expired:

1. Find the invitation in the Invitations list
2. Click the three-dot menu next to it
3. Select "Resend invitation"
4. A new invitation email is sent with a fresh expiration date

<Tip>
Resending an expired invitation automatically reactivates it with a new 7-day expiration.
</Tip>

### Canceling Invitations

Revoke an invitation before it's accepted:

1. Locate the pending invitation
2. Click the three-dot menu
3. Select "Cancel invitation"
4. Confirm the cancellation

## Seat Limits and Plans

### Understanding Seat Usage

Your plan determines how many team members can access your workspace:

<Note>
**Seat Limits by Plan:**
- **Free Plan**: 1 seat (owner only)
- **Hobby Plan**: 3 seats
- **Pro Plan**: 10 seats
- **Enterprise**: Custom seat count
</Note>

### Seat Usage Display

The Team Settings page shows:

```
Seats Used: 2 / 3
1 seat remaining
```

**What Counts as a Seat:**
- Active team members (Owner, Admins, Members)
- Pending invitations count against your limit
- Accepted invitations become active seats
- Canceled/expired invitations free up seats

### Reaching Seat Limit

When all seats are full:

- The "Invite" button is disabled
- A message appears: "You've reached your seat limit"
- An "Upgrade" button lets you increase capacity
- Cancel pending invitations to free up seats

<Tip>
Pending invitations count against your limit. Cancel unused invitations to free seats for new team members.
</Tip>

## Collaboration Features

### Conversation Participants

Team members working on conversations:

- **Auto-Added**: Sending a message automatically adds you as a participant
- **Visible to All**: All team members see who's involved
- **Participant List**: View all participants in the conversation sidebar

### Real-Time Presence

See when team members are active:

- **Online Indicators**: Green dot next to active members
- **Typing Indicators**: See when colleagues are typing responses
- **Last Seen**: View when team members last accessed the dashboard

### Internal Notes

Communicate privately within conversations:

- **Team-Only Messages**: Write notes only your team can see
- **Context Sharing**: Coordinate responses without confusing visitors
- **Marked with Lock Icon**: Easy to identify private messages

## Access Levels

### Organization-Wide Access

Admins have automatic access to:

- All current websites in the organization
- Any new websites created in the future
- Organization settings and billing (view-only for non-owners)

### Website-Specific Access

Members can be granted access to:

- Individual websites only
- No access to other websites in the organization
- No organization-level settings

<Note>
To give a Member access to all websites, promote them to Admin role.
</Note>

## Team Workflow Tips

### Assigning Conversations

While Cossistant doesn't have formal assignment:

- Use internal notes to indicate who's handling a conversation
- Check participant lists to see who's involved
- Look for typing indicators before jumping into a conversation

### Coverage During Off-Hours

Maximize your AI agent:

- Let AI handle routine questions 24/7
- Train your AI thoroughly for common scenarios
- Team members can follow up on AI conversations during business hours

### Handoffs Between Team Members

Transition conversations smoothly:

1. Write an internal note summarizing the context
2. Tag important details for the next person
3. Use conversation titles to indicate status
4. Mark conversations as resolved when done

## Upgrading for More Seats

When you need more team members:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Upgrade">
    Click the "Upgrade" button on the Team Settings page
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Plan">
    Choose a plan with sufficient seats for your team
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Payment">
    Enter payment details and confirm the upgrade
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Members">
    New seats are available immediately - start inviting
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

### Invitation Not Received

**Solutions:**
- Check spam/junk folder
- Verify correct email address was entered
- Resend the invitation
- Contact support if still not received

### Member Cannot Access Website

**Possible causes:**
- Member role without specific website access
- Invitation not yet accepted
- Account not fully activated

**Solutions:**
- Verify member accepted the invitation
- Check if member has correct role (Admin vs Member)
- Promote to Admin for organization-wide access

### Seat Count Seems Wrong

**Check these factors:**
- Pending invitations count against limit
- Expired invitations may not have cleared
- Cancel unused invitations to free seats
- Refresh the page to see updated count
