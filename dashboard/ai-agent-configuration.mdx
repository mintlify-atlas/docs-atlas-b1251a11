# AI Agent Configuration

Your AI agent is an intelligent assistant that automatically responds to customer questions based on your knowledge base. Configure its personality, behavior, and capabilities to deliver exceptional automated support.

## Getting Started with AI Agent

When you first set up Cossistant, you'll go through an onboarding flow to create your AI agent:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Provide Website Information">
    Enter your website URL and let Cossistant automatically extract brand information
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define Agent Personality">
    Choose your agent's name, tone, and goals
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate Base Prompt">
    Cossistant creates an optimized prompt based on your website and goals
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Knowledge Sources">
    Upload initial training data (web pages, files, FAQs)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Train Your Agent">
    Process knowledge to activate your AI agent
  </Step>
</Steps>

## AI Agent Settings

Access agent configuration from **AI Agent > Settings** in the dashboard.

### General Settings

Customize your agent's core identity:

#### Agent Name

- The name visitors see when your AI responds
- Common choices: Company name, "Support Bot", "AI Assistant"
- Can include emoji for personality (e.g., "Acme Helper 🤖")

#### Agent Description

- Internal note describing your agent's purpose
- Not visible to visitors
- Helpful for team reference and documentation

#### Base Prompt

The foundational instruction that defines your AI's personality:

```markdown
You are a helpful customer support assistant for Acme Corp, 
a B2B SaaS company providing project management software.

Your role is to:
- Answer questions about features, pricing, and technical issues
- Guide users through common workflows
- Escalate complex billing or account issues to human agents

Tone: Professional, friendly, and solution-oriented
Voice: Clear and concise - avoid jargon unless necessary
```

<Tip>
A well-crafted base prompt is crucial for consistent AI behavior. Be specific about tone, scope, and escalation criteria.
</Tip>

#### Agent Goals

Define 2-5 primary objectives for your AI:

- Resolve common technical issues
- Explain product features and pricing
- Qualify leads and schedule demos
- Guide users through onboarding
- Reduce support ticket volume

### AI Model Configuration

#### Model Selection

Choose the AI model powering your agent:

- **Claude 3.5 Sonnet** (Recommended): Best balance of speed, quality, and cost
- **GPT-4 Turbo**: OpenAI's powerful model
- **GPT-4o**: Latest OpenAI model with improved performance

<Note>
**Model Access by Plan:**
- **Free Plan**: Claude 3.5 Sonnet only
- **Paid Plans**: Access to all latest models
</Note>

#### Temperature

Control response creativity (0.0 - 1.0):

- **0.0 - 0.3**: Focused, consistent, deterministic (recommended for support)
- **0.4 - 0.6**: Balanced creativity and consistency
- **0.7 - 1.0**: More creative and varied responses

<Tip>
For customer support, use temperature 0.2-0.3 for reliable, factual responses.
</Tip>

#### Max Output Tokens

Limit response length:

- **Default: 1024 tokens** (~750 words)
- **Range: 256 - 4096 tokens**
- Lower values = shorter, more concise responses
- Higher values = more detailed explanations

### Active Status

Control whether your AI agent responds to conversations:

- **Active** (ON): AI automatically responds to new messages
- **Inactive** (OFF): AI does not respond - all conversations require human attention

<Warning>
When inactive, your AI agent will not respond to any visitors. Use this for testing or maintenance periods.
</Warning>

## Behavior Settings

Fine-tune how your AI agent behaves in conversations.

### Tool Invocation Budget

Limit how many actions the AI can take per response:

- **Default: 20 invocations**
- **Range: 5 - 50**
- Prevents runaway AI behavior
- Ensures timely responses

**What counts as a tool invocation:**
- Searching knowledge base
- Calling external APIs
- Using specialized functions

<Note>
Most responses use 2-5 invocations. Only complex queries approach the limit.
</Note>

### Training Status

Monitor your agent's training progress:

- **Idle**: No training in progress, agent is ready
- **Pending**: Training queued, will start shortly
- **Training**: Processing knowledge (shows percentage complete)
- **Completed**: Training finished successfully
- **Failed**: Error occurred during training (see error message)

### Training Intervals

Plan limits determine how often you can train:

<Note>
**Training Frequency by Plan:**
- **Free Plan**: Once per 24 hours
- **Hobby Plan**: Once per hour
- **Pro Plan**: Unlimited (no cooldown)
</Note>

### Training Readiness

Before the "Train" button becomes active:

- **Knowledge Updates**: System detects new or modified content
- **Cooldown Period**: Respects your plan's training interval
- **Button State**: 
  - Green = Ready to train
  - Gray = Waiting for cooldown or no changes detected
  - Shows next available training time

## Advanced Prompt Configuration

For power users, customize specific AI behaviors.

### Behavior Studio

Access from **AI Agent > Behavior** to customize:

#### Core Behaviors

- **Greeting Style**: How AI introduces itself
- **Escalation Detection**: When to hand off to humans
- **Response Format**: Structured vs. conversational
- **Clarification Strategy**: How AI asks for more information
- **Closing Messages**: How AI ends conversations

#### Preset Options

Each behavior includes presets:

- **Formal**: Professional, corporate tone
- **Casual**: Friendly, relaxed style
- **Concise**: Brief, to-the-point responses
- **Detailed**: Comprehensive explanations

<Tip>
Test different presets in real conversations to find what resonates with your audience.
</Tip>

### Prompt Studio

Advanced users can directly edit prompt documents:

- **Core Prompts**: Foundational instructions for AI behavior
- **System Prompts**: Technical instructions for AI capabilities
- **Override Defaults**: Customize any aspect of AI behavior

<Warning>
Editing prompts requires understanding of AI prompt engineering. Incorrect modifications can cause unexpected behavior.
</Warning>

## Testing Your AI Agent

Validate your agent's performance before going live:

### Preview Mode

Test your AI without affecting real visitors:

1. Open the widget preview on any training page
2. Ask questions as a visitor would
3. Evaluate response quality and accuracy
4. Iterate on knowledge base or prompts

### Test Questions

Ask these types of questions:

**Knowledge Coverage:**
- "What are your pricing plans?"
- "How do I reset my password?"
- "What features are included in Pro?"

**Edge Cases:**
- Typos and misspellings
- Vague or ambiguous questions
- Multiple questions in one message

**Escalation Triggers:**
- "I need to speak with a human"
- "This isn't working, I want a refund"
- "Can I talk to your manager?"

When your AI doesn't know something:

1. AI responds with "I don't have information about that"
2. AI offers to escalate to a human team member
3. Review the question and add relevant content to your knowledge base

</Tip>

## Danger Zone

### Deleting Your AI Agent

<Warning>
Deleting your AI agent is **permanent and irreversible**. All knowledge base entries, training data, and settings will be permanently deleted.
</Warning>

To delete your agent:

1. Scroll to the **Danger Zone** section at the bottom of Settings
2. Click "Delete Agent"
3. Confirm by typing your agent's name
4. Click "Permanently Delete"

What gets deleted:
- AI agent configuration
- All knowledge base entries (web sources, files, FAQs)
- Training data and embeddings
- Behavior customizations

What's preserved:
- Past conversations (conversation history remains)
- Visitor contact information
- Team member access

## Troubleshooting

### AI Not Responding

**Check these settings:**
- Verify AI agent is **Active** (toggle is ON)
- Ensure training status is **Completed**
- Check knowledge base has included content
- Confirm AI isn't paused for specific conversations

### Inaccurate Responses

**Improve accuracy:**
- Add more relevant knowledge sources
- Remove outdated or contradictory content
- Train agent after making changes
- Lower temperature for more consistent responses

### Training Failures

**Common causes:**
- Exceeded plan's knowledge base limits
- Invalid content format
- Empty knowledge entries
- Network timeouts

**Solutions:**
- Check plan limits and upgrade if needed
- Validate content format (markdown for files)
- Remove empty entries
- Try training again (temporary issues often resolve)

### Agent Ignoring Instructions

**Possible fixes:**
- Make base prompt more explicit and directive
- Use stronger language ("always", "never", "must")
- Simplify complex instructions
- Test with specific examples
