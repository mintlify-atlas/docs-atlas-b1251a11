# Knowledge Base Management

Your knowledge base is the foundation of your AI agent's intelligence. By adding relevant content from your website, files, and FAQs, you enable your AI to provide accurate, context-aware responses to customer questions.

## Knowledge Base Overview

The knowledge base consists of three main types of content:

  </Card>
  <Card title="Files" icon="file-text">

  <Card title="FAQs" icon="question-circle">

## Adding Web Sources

Automatically train your AI agent by crawling your website:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Web Sources">
    Go to **AI Agent > Training > Web** in the dashboard
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add URL">
    Click "Add URL" and enter your website URL (e.g., `https://example.com`)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Crawl Settings">
    - **Include Paths**: Specify URL patterns to crawl (optional)
    - **Exclude Paths**: Skip specific URLs or sections (e.g., `/admin`, `/login`)
    - **Crawl Limit**: Set maximum pages to crawl (based on your plan)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Crawling">
    Click "Add" to begin crawling. The system will discover and process pages automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Crawl Limits by Plan

<Note>
**Free Plan**: Up to 50 pages per website source  
**Hobby Plan**: Up to 1,000+ total pages  
**Pro Plan**: Up to 5,000+ total pages
</Note>

### Managing Web Sources

The **Web Sources** page displays a hierarchical tree view:

- **Domain-level organization**: All URLs grouped by domain
- **Folder structure**: Pages organized by URL path
- **Status indicators**: See crawl status (completed, in progress, failed)
- **Individual page controls**: Toggle pages on/off without re-crawling

#### Domain Tree View

```
📁 example.com (45 pages)
  📄 / - Homepage
  📁 /docs (12 pages)
    📄 /docs/getting-started
    📄 /docs/installation
  📁 /pricing (3 pages)
    📄 /pricing/plans
```

### Excluding Content

Use exclude patterns to skip irrelevant pages:

**Common exclusions:**
- `/admin/*` - Admin panels
- `/login` - Login pages
- `/cart/*` - Shopping cart
- `*?page=*` - Paginated URLs
- `/thank-you` - Thank you pages

<Tip>
Use wildcards (`*`) to match multiple paths: `/blog/*/comments/*` excludes all comment sections.
</Tip>

## Adding Files

Upload documents to provide detailed information:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Files">
    Go to **AI Agent > Training > Files** in the dashboard
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add File">
    Click "Add File" to upload or create content manually
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Method">
    - **Upload File**: Select `.md` or `.txt` files from your computer
    - **Manual Entry**: Write content directly in the editor
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Metadata">
    - **Title**: Descriptive name for the file
    - **Summary**: Optional brief description (helps AI understand context)
    - **Content**: Full markdown or text content
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Supported File Formats

- **Markdown (`.md`)**: Full formatting support including headers, lists, tables
- **Plain Text (`.txt`)**: Simple text documents

<Warning>
PDF, Word, and other binary formats require conversion to markdown or text before upload.
</Warning>

### File Best Practices

**Structure your content clearly:**
```markdown
# Product Documentation

## Overview
Our product helps you manage customer support efficiently.

## Key Features
- Real-time messaging
- AI-powered responses
- Team collaboration

## Pricing
We offer three plans: Free, Hobby, and Pro.
```

**Tips for effective files:**
- Use clear headings to organize information
- Keep content focused on specific topics
- Include keywords your customers might use
- Update files when information changes

## Creating FAQs

Add manual question-and-answer pairs for common inquiries:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to FAQs">
    Go to **AI Agent > Training > FAQ** in the dashboard
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add FAQ">
    Click "Add FAQ" to create a new entry
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Question">
    Write the question as customers would ask it
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide Answer">
    Write a clear, complete answer using markdown formatting
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click "Save" to add the FAQ to your knowledge base
  </Step>
</Steps>

### FAQ Example

**Question:**
```
How do I reset my password?
```

**Answer:**
```markdown
To reset your password:

1. Go to the login page
2. Click "Forgot Password?"
3. Enter your email address
4. Check your email for a reset link
5. Follow the link and create a new password

If you don't receive the email within 5 minutes, check your spam folder or contact support.
```

## Training Your AI Agent

After adding knowledge base content, train your AI agent to use it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review Changes">
    Check that all sources are marked as "Included" (toggle switch is on)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Train">
    Navigate to any training page and click the "Train" button
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for Processing">
    Training typically takes 2-5 minutes depending on content volume
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor Progress">
    A progress bar shows training status and completion percentage
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Training Status Indicators

- **Idle**: No training in progress
- **Pending**: Training queued, starting soon
- **Training**: Processing content and generating embeddings (shows progress %)
- **Completed**: Training finished successfully
- **Failed**: Training encountered an error (see error message)

<Note>
**Training Intervals by Plan:**
- **Free Plan**: Once per 24 hours
- **Hobby Plan**: Once per hour
- **Pro Plan**: Unlimited training
</Note>

### What Happens During Training?

1. **Content Extraction**: Text is extracted from all included sources
2. **Chunking**: Content is split into optimal-sized segments
3. **Embedding Generation**: AI creates vector representations of content
4. **Indexing**: Embeddings are indexed for fast retrieval
5. **Activation**: Your AI agent now uses this knowledge to answer questions

## Managing Knowledge Entries

### Viewing All Knowledge

Each training section (Web, Files, FAQ) shows:
- **Entry count**: Total number of items
- **Plan limits**: Current usage vs. plan limit
- **Last updated**: When content was last modified
- **Include/exclude toggle**: Enable or disable specific entries

### Editing Entries

**Web Sources:**
- Cannot edit crawled content directly (it reflects your live website)
- Can toggle pages on/off
- Can recrawl to update content

**Files:**
1. Click the edit icon next to any file
2. Modify title, summary, or content
3. Click "Save" to update
4. Train your AI to apply changes

**FAQs:**
1. Click the edit icon next to any FAQ
2. Update question or answer
3. Click "Save"
4. Train your AI to apply changes

### Deleting Entries

<Warning>
Deleting knowledge entries is permanent and cannot be undone.
</Warning>

1. Click the delete icon (trash can) next to the entry
2. Confirm deletion in the popup dialog
3. Train your AI to remove the content from its knowledge

### Including/Excluding Content

Control which entries the AI uses:

- **Included** (toggle on): AI uses this content when responding
- **Excluded** (toggle off): Content is stored but not used by AI

<Tip>
Exclude outdated content temporarily instead of deleting it. You can re-include it later if needed.
</Tip>

## Storage Limits

<Note>
**Storage Limits by Plan:**
- **Free Plan**: 10 MB total knowledge base
- **Hobby Plan**: 50 MB total knowledge base
- **Pro Plan**: 200 MB total knowledge base
</Note>

When approaching limits:
- Remove unnecessary content
- Exclude rarely-used pages
- Upgrade your plan for more storage

## Advanced Features

### Knowledge Preview

View exactly what your AI sees:

1. Click the preview icon next to any entry
2. See the processed content as the AI interprets it
3. Verify formatting and structure are correct

### Bulk Operations

**For Web Sources:**
- Select multiple pages using checkboxes
- Apply include/exclude to all selected
- Delete multiple entries at once

### Recrawling Websites

Update web content to reflect website changes:

1. Click the refresh icon next to a domain
2. The system re-crawls all pages for that source
3. New pages are added, removed pages are deleted
4. Modified pages are updated
5. Train your AI to apply the updates

<Tip>
Recrawl automatically when you update your website documentation or product pages.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

### Crawl Failures

**Problem**: Website pages won't crawl

**Solutions:**
- Verify the URL is publicly accessible (not behind a login)
- Check robots.txt doesn't block crawling
- Ensure pages don't require JavaScript to load content
- Try crawling a different URL from the same domain

### Training Failures

**Problem**: Training fails with an error

**Solutions:**
- Check if you've exceeded plan limits
- Verify content isn't empty or corrupted
- Remove extremely large files (split into smaller parts)
- Contact support if error persists

### AI Not Using Knowledge

**Problem**: AI gives generic answers instead of using your content

**Solutions:**
- Verify training completed successfully (status shows "Completed")
- Check entries are marked as "Included"
- Ensure content is relevant to the question being asked
- Try rephrasing content to match common customer questions
