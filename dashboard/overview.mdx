# Dashboard Overview

The Cossistant dashboard is your command center for managing customer conversations, configuring your AI agent, and gaining insights into visitor interactions. This powerful interface gives you complete control over your customer support operations.

## Key Sections

The dashboard is organized into several key areas, each designed to help you manage specific aspects of your customer support workflow:

## Navigation

The dashboard features a clean, intuitive navigation structure:

- **Left Sidebar**: Quick access to inbox filters (Open, Resolved, Archived, Spam)
- **Main Content Area**: Dynamic content based on your current view
- **Top Bar**: Website selector, settings, and user account menu

<Tip>
Use keyboard shortcuts to navigate quickly:
- Press `/` to focus the search bar
- Use arrow keys to navigate conversations
- Press `Esc` to close overlays
</Tip>

## Real-Time Updates

The dashboard automatically updates in real-time using WebSocket connections:

- **New Messages**: Instantly appear in your inbox with sound notifications (configurable)
- **Visitor Presence**: See when visitors are online or typing
- **Conversation Status**: Updates reflect immediately across all team members
- **AI Agent Actions**: Watch your AI agent respond to visitors in real-time

<Note>
All timestamps automatically adjust to your local timezone for convenience.
</Note>

## Dashboard Features

### Quick Stats

At a glance, monitor key metrics:
- Open conversations requiring attention
- Conversations resolved today
- Active visitors on your site
- AI agent response rate

### Conversation Filters

Organize conversations by status:
- **Open**: Active conversations requiring human attention
- **Resolved**: Successfully closed conversations
- **Archived**: Older conversations removed from active view
- **Spam**: Filtered suspicious or unwanted conversations

### Team Collaboration

When working with a team:
- See who's viewing or responding to conversations
- Avoid duplicate responses with presence indicators
- Share context through internal notes (private messages)
- Assign conversations to specific team members

## Mobile Responsiveness

The dashboard adapts seamlessly to different screen sizes:
- **Desktop**: Full sidebar and multi-pane layout
- **Tablet**: Collapsible sidebar with optimized spacing
- **Mobile**: Stack navigation with swipe gestures
